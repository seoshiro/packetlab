## New reproduced defect and repair

**P3 — native modal background scroll leaks at the boundary.** On production Chromium at 320×844, open **Replay**, scroll the dialog to its lower edge, then wheel downward over its right padding. The dialog stays open but the document beneath changes from **453px to 2453px**. Expected: scrolling stays inside the dialog and the original page position remains stable; closing restores normal page scrolling.

| Before | After | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Modal uses default scroll chaining; background moves under the overlay | `src/refinements.css:241`: `html:has(dialog[open]) { overflow: hidden; }`; open dialogs contain overscroll | Separate page scroll isolation from native focus isolation. Preserve scrolling within long dialogs and unlock it on Escape. |
| [Real production capture before](followup-2026-10-24/before-modal.png) | [Real repaired capture after](followup-2026-10-24/after-modal.png) | No generated image; synthetic demonstration data only. |

Regression: `tests/e2e/uiux-modal-scroll.spec.ts`, 320px and 1440px. Each case checks no document movement at the dialog boundary, internal scrolling when overflowing, Escape/opener focus, and restored page scrolling.
